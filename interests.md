# Must read
- PRMT5 and MTAP-deleted cancers (MTA-cooperative PRMT5 inhibitors, MRTX1719, navlimetostat, vopimetostat, AMG 193, AZD3470, MAT2A, CDKN2A/9p21 co-deletion, SDMA)
- KIF18A and chromosomal instability as a vulnerability (KIF18A inhibitors, sovilnesib, VLS-1488, aneuploidy, whole-genome doubling)
- SHOC2 and RAS–MAPK signaling nodes (SHOC2–MRAS–PP1C complex, SHP2, PTPN11, RAF dimerization)
- Brain-penetrant targeted therapy and brain metastases (blood–brain barrier, Kp,uu, P-gp, BCRP, intracranial response, CNS metastases, brain-penetrant inhibitors in glioblastoma)

# Also relevant
- New druggable cancer targets and their validation (novel dependencies, first-in-class inhibitors, target deconvolution)
- RAS pathway inhibitors (KRAS G12C, KRAS G12D, pan-RAS, RAS(ON), MEK, ERK)
- Synthetic lethality and DNA damage response targets (PARP, WRN, POLQ, PKMYT1, ATR, USP1, paralog dependencies)
- Epigenetic and RNA-processing targets (PRMT1, CARM1, EZH2, menin, splicing factors)
- Targeted therapy, oncogenic drivers and drug resistance in PDAC, NSCLC, esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC) and GBM — not epidemiology or prognostic signatures
- CRISPR and functional genomic screens (DepMap, dependency, synthetic lethal screens) — only when they nominate druggable targets
- Small-molecule drug discovery (structure-based design, cryo-EM/crystal structures, molecular glues, PROTACs, covalent inhibitors)
- Clinical pharmacology and biomarkers (dose optimization, exposure–response, ctDNA response, companion diagnostics)
- Early clinical trials of targeted therapy in solid tumors (first-in-human, phase 1 dose escalation); phase 2/3 only in PDAC, NSCLC, ESCC or GBM
- Tumor immunology and immunotherapy (PD-1, checkpoint inhibitors, MHC-I, T-cell infiltration) — only in PDAC, NSCLC, ESCC or GBM, or combined with targeted therapy

# Not relevant
- News, editorials, commentaries, perspectives and meeting reports
- Cancer epidemiology, screening, disparities and survivorship
- Prognostic gene signatures, nomograms, and lncRNA/circRNA/miRNA sponge axis studies
- Radiomics, AI pathology and diagnostic imaging models
- Hematologic malignancies and CAR-T, unless the paper involves a target listed above
- Hormone receptor biology in breast and prostate cancer (ER, AR)
- Bioinformatics tools and methods benchmarks without a therapeutic finding
